## Acceptance criteria
- AC-3 — issue(userId) returns an opaque token that read(token) resolves to the user until expiry; revoke(token) and revokeAll(userId) invalidate immediately
- AC-4 — Sessions persist in Postgres and survive a process restart; expiry is 30 days sliding, refreshed at most once per hour
- AC-5 — The cookie is Secure, HttpOnly, SameSite=Lax, named __Host-session
